This piece plays that recording into a chip designed by Federico Corradi: sixteen analog spiking neurons on one piece of silicon, three point six by five point two millimetres. Each neuron is a circuit, like this one: a capacitor holds the membrane voltage, a transistor leaks it away, and a comparator watches for the threshold. It has no clock and computes no numbers. It answers in continuous time, in physics. And it never answers identically. No two neurons, born or made, ever do.
